## Broader poster-guide recommendations

- Clear visual hierarchy: red **Problem**, bold project definition, colored feature bullets, blue Method/Advantage/Demonstration/Conclusion labels.
- Concise method: calibration, filtering and USB state updates.
- Two-sentence Problem and SmartRail sections add context without reducing the required 54 pt description size.
- Concrete demonstration scope: two physical inputs and 1,294 simulated seats, totaling 1,296 seats in 12 logical cars.
- Qualitative conclusion: a shared display combines real and simulated seat states.
- Repository/contact line: intentionally removed from the poster at the user's request; no email or substitute contact details added.
- No invented quantitative evaluation, accuracy percentage, measured boarding improvement, external collaboration or hardware test report.
- Actual photos and app screenshots only; no generated image is presented as the physical prototype.
